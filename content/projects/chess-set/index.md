I designed and fabricated a custom chess set from October 2023 to February 2024, combining digital modelling with additive and subtractive manufacturing.

## Process

- Modelled custom chess pieces in Shapr3D and Blender.
- Manufactured and tested prototypes with 3D printing.
- Designed a personalized chessboard in Inkscape and fabricated it with laser cutting tools.
- Refined geometry and print settings to improve dimensional accuracy and structural stability.

## Tools

- Shapr3D
- Blender
- Inkscape
- 3D printing
- Laser cutting
